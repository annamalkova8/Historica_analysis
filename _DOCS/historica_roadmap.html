Risks to watch

Four people, two of them building. Sequencing matters more than ambition.

  • One analyst, three jobs: the business analyst + LLM engineer carries the map pipeline, interview synthesis and academics. Cap academics at about a day a week and co-write the paper with the university partner.
  • Maps capacity is shared: 13 periods × ~1 week is about a quarter of full-time work, but the same person also runs interviews and academics, so it is planned as two batches (Q1, Q2). Sparse early periods may need more than a week; Q3 is the buffer.
  • Paid launch needs a legal entity: a payment provider and VAT need one. The decision has to land by Q2 2027 or Q4 launch slips.
  • "Published" lags "submitted": a journal paper started in 2027 will likely publish in 2028. A conference proceedings paper counts and fits the same timeline.
  • Freelance UX availability: book interview and re-test windows ahead of time.
