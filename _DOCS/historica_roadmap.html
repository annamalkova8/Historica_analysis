Risks to watch

Three people, no incorporated entity — focus matters.

  • Scope vs. capacity: two products and outreach with a team of 3 — keep each quarter to one headline per track.
  • Data trust: one visible error in a famous border can cost credibility; sources and review come before coverage.
  • Beta stall: without publish-step fixes, builder users don't become publishers.
  • Funding gap: grant decisions arrive late; legal form (nonprofit vs. startup) blocks some programmes.
